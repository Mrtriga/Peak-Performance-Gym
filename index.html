About Peak Performance

Built by coaches. Run for members.

Peak Performance Fitness opened in 2014 with one belief: results come from real coaching, not just equipment. Every program on our floor is designed and supervised by certified specialists, not templated off a franchise manual.

Our Mission

Give every member — beginner to competitive athlete — a clear, coached path to their strongest, healthiest self.

Our Vision

To be the training ground Austin trusts for strength, longevity, and a culture that keeps people coming back.
